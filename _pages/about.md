Hi everyone!

I’m Alexander Dalheimer, a PhD student in the ERC-funded <a href='https://erc-partisan.github.io'>PARTISAN</a> project, led by <a href='https://www.wagnermarkus.net'>Markus Wagner</a>. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Politics and Administration from the <a href='https://www.polver.uni-konstanz.de/en/'>University of Konstanz</a> and a Master's degree in Political Science from the <a href='https://politik.uni-koeln.de/en/'>University of Cologne</a>.

My research focuses on the origins of partisan conflict and affective polarization. I investigate why supporters of different parties increasingly dislike each other across many liberal democracies, exploring both micro- and macro-level explanations. In particular, I study the roles of partisan meta-perceptions, political identity and policy disagreements, the influence of radical-right parties, and the effects of stereotypes.

I primarily work with quantitative methods, drawing on a variety of data sources. Thanks to the <a href='https://erc-partisan.github.io'>PARTISAN</a> project, I have the opportunity to collect and analyze cross-national survey data from more than ten countries and to run survey experiments. I also employ quasi-experimental and computational methods to answer my research questions with rigor and creativity.
